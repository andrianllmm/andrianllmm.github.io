Aklish is a web app that crowdsources <a href="https://www.ethnologue.com/language/akl" target="_blank">Aklanon</a>-English translations.
It was initially developed as a <abbr title="Senior High School">SHS</abbr> Research Capstone Project to help preserve and enrich
the Aklanon language, my mother tongue, which is classified as a Low-resource language (LRL).[^1]
The platform lets users contribute translations and has since grown to include dictionaries, spellcheckers, leaderboards, and word games.

## Goals

- **Expand language resources**: create reliable, accessible resources to help grow the language.
- **Encourage community collaboration**: let native speakers and learners take part in translating and improving content.
- **Support learning**: make it easier for people practice and learn the language.

### Quality Control Strategies

#### Voting System

Users can evaluate translations by either upvoting (+1) or downvoting (-1) them.

<Image src={voting} alt="Voting System" class="w-full max-w-80" data-zoomable />

#### Dictionaries

Provides users with words and their definitions, examples, related terms, and other properties
to help in maintaining consistency in their translations.
The dictionaries were created by parsing XML version of the <a href="https://ibiblio.org/webster/" target="_blank">GCIDE</a>
dictionary and a PDF <a href="https://archive.org/details/ERIC_ED145704" target="_blank">Aklanon dictionary</a>.

#### Proofreaders (Spellcheckers)

Highlights potential errors in translations, such as misspellings, by referencing the dictionary.
It suggests likely corrections, which users can choose to accept or ignore.
The spellchecker uses the <a href="https://pypi.org/project/symspellpy" target="_blank">SymSpellPy</a>
library, along with <a href="https://lemminflect.readthedocs.io" target="_blank">LemmInlect</a> and <a href="https://github.com/andrianllmm/aklanon-stemmer">AklStemmer</a>
libraries English and Aklanon word stemming to under the hood.

## Effectiveness

Through a comprehensive assessment of its functionality, performance, accessibility, adherence to modern standards (AMS),
search engine optimization (SEO), usability, and engagement, Aklish was determined to be functional (82.01%), perform well (81.33%),
be accessible (96.79%), adhere to modern standards (100%), be search engine optimized (99.21%), be usable (61.29%),
and relatively low in engagement based on several metrics.[^2]
