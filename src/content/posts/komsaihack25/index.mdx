## About the competition

_Komsai Hack 2025: Democratech Hackathon_ is a hackathon organized by <a href="https://www.komsai.org" target="_blank">Komsai.org</a>
that invites college students from Western Visayas
to create tech-driven solutions that enhance democratic processes.

Participants are tasked with brainstorming, designing, and developing applications that promote civic engagement, ensure election integrity, and improve accessibility in governance.

## How it turned out

We developed a decentralized election system that uses blockchain technology to ensure transparency and security.
