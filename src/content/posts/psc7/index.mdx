## About the competition

The Philippine Startup Challenge (PSC) is a nationwide startup pitching competition
organized by the <abbr title="Department of Information and Communications Technology">DICT</abbr>
to encourage Filipino students to build tech-enabled solutions. PSC7, held in
2022, had regional rounds leading up to national finals, where teams pitched
their ideas to ICT experts and industry mentors.

## How it turned out

Our team pitched _MATHuto_, a mobile app designed to help preschool and elementary students
strengthen their numeracy skills through games and interactive exercises.
The app includes guided lessons, practice sets, quizzes, and progress tracking, with features that work offline
for students with limited internet access.
